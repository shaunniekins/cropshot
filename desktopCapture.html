Cropshot

Record your screen, talk over it.

Your screen, your voice and a bubble of you, saved as a video file.

  1. What do you want to make?

  2. Pick a screen, window or tab next. With an area, you drag over the part to keep before it saves.

  3. Adds a round bubble of you to the corner of the video.

  5. Say something: the bar moves when this mic hears you.

Files save to Chrome's downloads folder. Nothing is uploaded.

What happens next

    Settings

    Where files go

    Videos (.webm) and screenshots (.png) save to Chrome's downloads folder. To choose a folder every time, turn on "Ask where to save each file before downloading" in Chrome.

    Camera and microphone access

    If Chrome blocked access, allow it again in the site settings.

    Select the area to keep

    Drag over the screenshot. Download saves only that area, or the whole screenshot if nothing is selected.
